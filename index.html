How it works

Three steps. No login. No paperwork.

Run the tool. Show the code to your bank. Get paid. That's it.

Step 01

Drop your URL.

We crawl your public pages — products, pricing, footer disclosures — to understand what you actually sell. No backend access, no permissions, no scraping behind a login.

Step 02

We classify the revenue.

Our model maps your offering to one of the ~300 codes in the RBI FED Master Direction Annexure. Each candidate gets a confidence score and an evidence trail.

Step 03

Hand it to your AD bank.

Copy the code into your invoice or your bank's inward remittance portal. Final attribution rests with your AD bank, but you're 95% of the way there.

HS Codes are for physical goods at customs. SAC Codes are for GST classification within India. Export Purpose Codes (the P-codes) are specifically for the RBI's balance-of-payments reporting on foreign-exchange receipts.
